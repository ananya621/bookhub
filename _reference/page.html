Frequently Asked Questions

Most trips today begin the same way: dozens of tabs, conflicting recommendations, and hours spent trying to figure out what is actually worth your time.

Spots exists to make that process more thoughtful, efficient, and aligned with what you value.

We help narrow down the overwhelming number of options into a smaller set of places, stays, and experiences that genuinely fit your pace, preferences, and travel style. Alongside practical planning, we bring a strong point of view shaped by design, atmosphere, hospitality, and cultural context.

The result is not just a more organized trip, but one that feels more intentional and enjoyable from beginning to end.

Our process is simple.

  1. Understanding your trip
    We start by learning about your travel goals, preferences, budget, pace, who you’re traveling with, and what matters most for this trip.
  2. Designing the itinerary
    We create a custom itinerary around your needs, including hotels, transportation, restaurants, experiences, and the rhythm of each day.
  3. Handling the details
    Once the itinerary is confirmed, we help coordinate the bookings and key details. During your trip, we’re also available online if you need support along the way.

Each journey is priced based on its unique scope and scale.

  • Planning & Design Fees: Professional design fees start from $500 per journey.
  • Travel Costs: Travel expenses are paid directly to suppliers for full transparency and are not included in the initial design fee.

We begin with a complimentary 30-minute conversation to explore your vision. A deposit is required to initiate the design process.

Because each journey is designed and booked specifically for you, cancellation terms vary depending on the suppliers involved.

We outline all policies clearly before any bookings are made. When plans change, we work with you to adjust the journey where possible.
