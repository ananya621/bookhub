Book Hub — Admin

Eleven screens for the person running the site: queues for reviews, accounts and book requests, a user directory, a safeguarding queue, a trash with a 14-day window, and the four dialogs. Same Pulp system as the reader app, deliberately plainer.

RULESHow admin differs from the reader app
Rule 01

Destructive actions are never the primary button. Orange is Approve. Delete and Ban are outlined, and both confirm before acting.

Rule 02

Every queue is one list, newest first, with the whole item visible. No expanding rows — you cannot judge a review you have to click to read.

Rule 03

Same four state colours: lime approved, red removed or declined, pink awaiting you, blue navigation.

01This is a back office, not a product. No hero type, no imagery, no marketing copy — density and legibility win over personality here.
02Access is a role on the user record, checked server-side. A hidden URL is not access control.

Safeguarding

Reports where a reader said they were worried about someone's safety. Kept out of the ordinary queues so they never wait behind spam.

Open FROM quiet_reader_11 · ABOUT A REVIEW ON “A LONG WAY DOWN” · 4 HOURS AGO

they said something about not wanting to be here anymore and it scared me

Open FROM zeni_reads · ABOUT THE READER kofi_a · YESTERDAY

he keeps asking people in reviews how old they are and where they go to school

01The fifth report reason (“something that worries me about their safety”) routes here, never into the review queue. A disclosure must not sit behind fourteen spam reports.
02It gets a red card at the top of G1, above the three pink queues — the only thing on the dashboard that outranks them.
03“Mark as actioned” means you did something outside this app: told a safeguarding lead, contacted a school, escalated. It is not a moderation verdict, and the wording says so.
04In the build these should also email you immediately. A queue you check on Fridays is not a safeguarding process.

EVERY DESTRUCTIVE ACTION HAS ONE OF THESE: DELETE REVIEW, DELETE ACCOUNT, BAN. THE CONFIRM IS RED-FILLED, CANCEL IS PLAIN.
01All four are the same dialog shell — kicker, title, body, actions — so there is one component to build, not four.
02A dialog taller than the screen must scroll and keep its buttons reachable. The ban dialog is the one that hits this on a laptop.
BEFORE YOU BUILD THE ADMIN
  1. Roles, not routes. Put an is_admin flag on the user and enforce it in row-level security. Hiding the URL protects nothing.
  2. Soft delete. Mark reviews and accounts as removed rather than destroying rows, so a mistake is recoverable and Undo is possible.
  3. Log who did what. One table: actor, action, target, timestamp, reason. You will want it the first time a parent emails you.
  4. One admin is a single point of failure. If you are away for a week, a reported review sits there. Decide now whether that is acceptable for v1 — it probably is, but know it.
  5. Notify on decline. The reader's requests screen and your decline reason are the same field. Wire the email or the in-app notice at the same time, or the reason is written for nobody.
