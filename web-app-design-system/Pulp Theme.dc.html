RULESFour accents, four jobs
#FF4D00
Orange = go

The one primary action on a screen. Nothing else is ever orange — that is what makes it obvious.

#1B3BFF
Blue = links

Anything that navigates: links, selected filters, cover fields, “Want to Read”.

#C6F24E
Lime = done

Finished, read, saved, confirmed. Always with ink text on it — never cream.

#FF3D9A
Pink = live

Happening now or yours: currently reading, new, your own review.

01Ink becomes the ground and cream becomes every outline — the structure is identical to P3, which is what makes this a variant rather than a second design.
02Lime and pink blocks keep ink text, so they stay readable on the dark ground. Blue takes cream text instead.
03Body copy drops to a warm grey (#9C8B76) rather than pure cream — full-strength cream on ink vibrates at paragraph size.
BEFORE YOU COMMIT TO THIS
  1. Four accents need the rules written down. The moment someone uses lime for a heading “because it looks nice”, the system is gone. Colour = state, never decoration.
  2. Anton once per screen. It is a display face. Two Antons on one page and neither is special.
  3. Check contrast on lime and pink. Both need ink text, never cream. Orange on cream passes for large text and buttons but not for body copy — use ink.
  4. Real covers will fight this. Book jackets bring their own colours. That is why cards stay cream and outlines stay black — the frame has to be neutral enough to hold anything.
  5. Keep the wireframes in Industry. Quiet wireframes are easier to critique; this theme is for the built thing.
